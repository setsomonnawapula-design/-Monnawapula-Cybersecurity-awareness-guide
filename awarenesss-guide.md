# Cybersecurity Awareness Guide
## 1. What is phishing?
It is the lowering of users by making legitimate looking websites that lures users to press the links or websites
Example: Please update your school password in the next 30 minutes with the link provided below
How to avoid
Know the legitimate channels that are safe and can be used to communicate with users
Avoid clicking links. If the URL does not match the email
## prevention methods
use strong passwords that include numbers, capital letters and symbols
Do not use the same password for all accounts
The use of MFA as a second layer of protection
## Updating systems
Update systems regularly
Enable the use of anti-viruses
Patch systems with a high/critical CVSS score
## recomendations
think before you click
do not share your credentials
use strong passwords and MFA
